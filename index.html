How it works

Three steps. Zero typing for your parent.

1

You add keys remotely

Hospital portal, pension site, pharmacy account — add them from your own phone, in the Guardian screen.

2

They sync privately

Everything travels as encrypted payloads through iCloud CloudKit under your family code. We have no servers.

3

Parent taps one tile

The key is copied and the website opens. No forms, no typing — the credential never appears on screen.
